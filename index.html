About Vipul Swami

Where passion meets mastery

Vipul Swami is a celebrated fine artist and educator with over 8+ years of experience nurturing young talents and adults. His teaching style blends traditional techniques with creative expression, ensuring every student finds their unique artistic voice. As the founder of Morya Drawing & Painting Classes, Vipul Sir has mentored hundreds of successful students, art competition winners, and aspiring designers.
